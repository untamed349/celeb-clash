Player 1: A/D move, W jump (twice), S drop through, F strike, G special. Player 2: arrows move, K strike, L special. Knock your rival off the stage. Damage % makes hits launch farther. One weapon at a time drifts down from the sky: a crate. Grab it and it becomes your character's own weapon until it breaks. The next weapon drops after one is picked up. Esc or P pauses. On a phone, play in landscape with the on-screen buttons.
